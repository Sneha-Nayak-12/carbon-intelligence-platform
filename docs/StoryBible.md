# Story Bible: The Carbon Journey

This document captures the narrative timeline, pacing, and emotional arcs of the interactive documentary.

---

## 1. Core Philosophy

CarbonOS is not just software—it is the digital orchestrator of a planetary cycle. To communicate this, the user does not read feature lists; they follow the lifecycle of **one molecule of carbon** as it transforms from an atmospheric threat into a trusted, permanent environmental asset.

```
ATMOSPHERIC GAS (Chapter 1)
       │
       ▼
PYROLYSIS SOLID (Chapter 2)
       │
       ▼
TELEMETRY AUDITED (Chapter 3)
       │
       ▼
MINTED ASSET (Chapter 4)
       │
       ▼
VAULT RETIREMENT (Chapter 5)
```

## 2. Emotional Arc Timeline

- **0–5 seconds (Stillness)**:
  - *Viewer State*: Calm, open, and slightly awed.
  - *Scene*: Silent cosmic void. No UI, no text. Just the horizon of the Earth shifting slowly.
- **5–12 seconds (Curiosity)**:
  - *Viewer State*: Eager to explore.
  - *Scene*: A single molecule begins to glow green. It falls slowly toward the soil.
- **Scroll Segment 10% - 30% (Transmutation)**:
  - *Viewer State*: Focused on physical process.
  - *Scene*: The molecule goes through intense heat inside the reactor, solidifying.
- **Scroll Segment 30% - 50% (Trust)**:
  - *Viewer State*: Reassured by mathematical verification.
  - *Scene*: Auditor signatures, telemetry lines, and network consensus seal the record.
- **Scroll Segment 50% - 80% (Fulfillment)**:
  - *Viewer State*: Clarity.
  - *Scene*: The asset is retired and locked, showing real carbon offset success.
